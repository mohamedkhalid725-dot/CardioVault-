CardioVault Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026

CardioVault is a clinical notebook application designed for healthcare professionals to organize cardiology and critical-care records. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the application.

1. Information You Provide

  • Account information: If you use account-based sign-in, CardioVault may receive basic account information provided by the authentication provider, such as your account identifier, email address, and display name.
  • Clinical information: Information that you enter into the application, including patient records, clinical observations, laboratory results, medications, procedures, notes, and related records.
  • Application data: App settings and information needed to maintain your local clinical notebook and synchronization state.

2. Local Storage and Cloud Synchronization

CardioVault is designed with a local-first approach. Clinical information can be stored on your device so that core notebook functions can work offline.

If you use the application's cloud synchronization features, relevant application data is synchronized to the CardioVault cloud workspace associated with your account. Cloud synchronization is intended to keep your authorized devices and workspace data consistent.

3. Authentication and Third-Party Services

CardioVault uses Google/Firebase services for authentication and cloud functionality. These services may process information required to authenticate your account and provide the requested synchronization functionality, subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

4. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide the application's features, including maintaining your clinical notebook, authenticating your account, synchronizing authorized data, generating reports, and improving reliability and security.

5. Sharing and Sale of Information

CardioVault does not sell your personal or clinical information. CardioVault does not intentionally make your clinical records publicly available. Data may be processed by the third-party infrastructure providers required to provide authentication, storage, synchronization, and other requested technical services.

6. Security

CardioVault is designed with security and privacy in mind, including local-first storage and protected authentication/cloud access. However, no software, device, or online service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Responsibility

You are responsible for using the application in accordance with applicable laws, professional requirements, institutional policies, and patient-privacy obligations. Do not enter information that you are not authorized to store or process.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Local information may remain on your device until you remove it or clear the application's data. Cloud-synchronized information may remain in the associated cloud workspace according to the application's data-management and account processes. If you need assistance with deletion of account-associated cloud data, contact the CardioVault developer through the developer account associated with the application.

9. Children's Privacy

CardioVault is intended for professional clinical use and is not directed to children.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application or its data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a new version is published.

11. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, contact the CardioVault developer through the developer account or support channel associated with the application listing.
